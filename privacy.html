Arrow Pets: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Arrow Pets ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect ourselves

None that identifies you. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (level, coins, hints, skins, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
  • app interactions (for example, ad views and taps),
  • diagnostic and performance information (for example, crash logs).

This data is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent encrypted. How Google uses it is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics and ad measurement

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Meta (Facebook) App Events to understand how the game is played and to measure our ad campaigns. They may collect:

  • anonymous gameplay events (for example "level started", "level completed", "hint used"),
  • app instance and device identifiers, including the advertising ID,
  • device information (model, Android version, language) and approximate location from the IP address,
  • ad revenue events.

Firebase Remote Config is used to adjust game settings (difficulty, rewards) without an update.

These services are only switched on after the consent check: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only if you agree in the consent message, and you can withdraw that at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. See Google's privacy policy and Meta's data policy.

Deleting your data

  • Game progress is stored only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Pets → Storage → Clear data, and it is gone.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Data collected with the old ID can then no longer be linked to you.
  • Data held by Google and Meta (AdMob, Firebase, Meta App Events) is kept under their own policies, linked above. We hold nothing that identifies you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete. On Facebook or Instagram you can also disconnect app activity from your account under Your activity off Meta technologies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game shows up to 3 reminders a day (for example when your daily gift is ready), never at night. They are created on your phone from your saved progress; nothing is sent to a server. Turn them off any time in Settings → Daily reminders in the game, or in your phone's notification settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
